Settings (not complicated)

Pebble supports a phone config page via Clay in src/pkjs/index.js. Two options, stored on the phone and sent to the watch:

  • Temperature unit: Fahrenheit / Celsius
  • Time format: 12-hour / 24-hour

Watch reads prefs on launch and when settings change; formats time and temp strings in C. No extra hardware APIs needed.
